A clock comparison is sensitive to the gravitational redshift. Two identical clocks at heights differing by $\Delta h$ in a field of strength $g$ tick at rates that differ by the fraction $\Delta\nu/\nu = g\,\Delta h/c^2$, which for $\Delta h = 1\,\mathrm{cm}$ on Earth is about $10^{-18}$. Optical clocks resolve this.
