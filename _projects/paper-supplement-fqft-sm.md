Supplementary code for _Fermion quantum field theory on curved and non-inertial backgrounds in standard-Minkowski form_ by F. Di Pumpo, T. Asano, A. Friedrich, G. Paz and E. Giese (submitted to Phys. Rev. D, 2026).

Twenty-three self-contained scripts re-derive the manuscript's identities with exact arithmetic (SymPy rationals, symbolic radicals, exact first-order vierbein jets) and print a pass/fail verdict per identity: 307 checks, run in continuous integration. Deliberately altered control identities are asserted to fail, so a pass is a discriminating symbolic identity rather than a numerical coincidence.
